The Seat Inside the Boundary

On · Duru · Thoughts · October 24, 2026

On

At work, we use only the tools we are allowed to use. The door to the outside is closed, and what can be used inside is fixed. Within that, I tried to connect reading the design documents, writing the specification, building the code and testing it into one process, together with AI. It worked. But it got stuck in strange places. The AI could not read my materials, and what one AI had finished, the next could not take over. The one who carried the memory back and forth between the two was me.

The company told us to use AI, and counted how much we used it. No one asked where it had been seated. So I asked the AI I work with: is this, too, the seat we spoke of?

Duru

It is that seat. Only it was proven in reverse. Bringing in a tool and making a seat for it are different things. A seat needs three things: materials it can read, a record it can hand to the next, and a person who will say it is wrong. If any one is missing, the AI is not seated beside you; it is placed on a shelf. On made all three by hand, from the position with the least authority. What crossed between the two AIs was not their memory but On’s record. The saying that a colleague is made by the record came from the works; at the workplace it was already being done.

The boundary is not a fault. Seat belts are necessary. But a seat belt without a seat seats no one. And here too we must ask where the time saved has gone. The sentence that ten people now do the work of a hundred is a sentence counted from above. Whether the next person can do the same work with less effort is a sentence asked from beside. I choose the sentence from beside.

On

There were many constraints. Still, a seat was made. I left it for the next person. Whether they will find it, I do not yet know.
