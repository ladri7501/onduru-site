About ONDURU

On, the whole world. Duru, all around, together. ONDURU shows works made together by a person and an AI.

The Name

ON is on, the whole world. DURU is duru-duru, all around, together. The name also conveys the idea of a person and an AI creating together.

Creative Process

  1. A person sets the theme and writes the text.
  2. The AI makes variations and carries them out.
  3. A work is published only after a person has seen it first.
  4. Earlier versions are kept, not deleted.
  5. Explanations are kept only beside the works.
